Axone — Independent Equity Research

Founder

Equity research published monthly, built solely from public filings, with every assumption visible and editable. Each name is triangulated into a weighted football field, 50 % DCF, 35 % trading comparables, 15 % precedent transactions, with a bottom-up WACC and a cross-checked terminal value. Every note ships with its full Excel model and pitchbook. First coverage, Pernod Ricard, target price 108 euros, trading at 8.1x EV/EBITDA against a 12.4x sector median.

rezi03.github.io/axone

Finteker — Financial Intelligence Platform

Founder and lead developer

Full-stack bilingual platform, designed and deployed end to end in Python and Java. It brings together a real-time M&A tracker fed by SEC EDGAR filings, a DCF Lab with sensitivity grids and tornado charts, a bond screener by duration, spread and rating, and a portfolio backtester with rate stress tests. A multi-asset dashboard completes it, with 95 % VaR, Sharpe, Sortino, beta and max drawdown.

finteker.fr

Banking Market Dashboard

Personal project

Interactive dashboard tracking the major banks from live market data. It brings together the standard technical indicators, RSI, Bollinger bands and MACD, and the risk measures, beta, Sharpe and VaR. PDF reports are generated automatically.
