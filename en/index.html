Equity research published monthly, built solely from public filings, with every assumption visible and editable. Each name is triangulated into a weighted football field, 50 % DCF, 35 % trading comparables, 15 % precedent transactions, with a bottom-up WACC and a cross-checked terminal value. Full-stack bilingual platform, designed and deployed end to end in Python and Java.
